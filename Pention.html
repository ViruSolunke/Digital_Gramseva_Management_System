Govt. Welfare Scheme

Old Age
Pension Scheme

A social security initiative providing monthly financial assistance to senior citizens belonging to BPL households to ensure a dignified life.

Eligibility Criteria

Age 60+ Years

Must be 60 years or above as per Aadhar.

Economic Status

Applicant must belong to a BPL household.

Required Documents
Aadhar Card (Age Proof)
Bank Passbook Copy
BPL Ration Card

Registration Process

Download the form below, fill the details and submit to your local Block Development Office (BDO).
