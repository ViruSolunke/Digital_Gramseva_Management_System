Welfare Schemes & Programs

Empowering every household with direct government benefits.

PM Awas Yojana

Financial assistance for construction of permanent houses for homeless villagers. Includes ₹1.2 Lakh subsidy.

Eligibility: BPL Families / Homeless

Required: Ration Card, Income Certificate

Apply Online

PM-Kisan Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in three installments directly into farmer bank accounts.

Eligibility: Land-owning Farmer Families

Required: 7/12 Extract, Bank Passbook

MGNREGA Work

Guaranteed 100 days of wage employment in a financial year for adult members of rural households.

Eligibility: Any adult villager

Required: Job Card, Aadhar Card

Request Work

Ayushman Bharat

Free health insurance coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care.

Eligibility: SECC Data listed families

Required: Golden Card / PM Letter

State Scholarship

Financial support for students belonging to SC/ST/OBC and Minority categories for higher studies.

Eligibility: Students (Income < 2.5L)

Required: Bonafide, Caste Certificate

Old Age Pension

Monthly financial pension for senior citizens (above 60 years) to live a life of dignity.

Eligibility: Age 60+ (BPL)

Required: Age Proof, Bank Account
